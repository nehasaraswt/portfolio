Immersive design, built from students' own experiences

Master's course · Dhirubhai Ambani University, Gandhinagar · Course design and teaching · 202618 days · experience, specification, build · all 20 students finished
the memory and the world, at the same time
20master's students from architecture, film, engineering and more

The course was planned around the pols and stepwells of Ahmedabad. It ended up built from the students' own experiences, and that turned out well.

The commute was difficult to arrange, and we had only a few sets of recording gear, so the site visits would have made the experience repetitive.

Most students had no training in sketching. So they wrote specifications instead of drawing them, and built their worlds in Blender and Unity.

Three stages
Experience
Down memory lane.Students recalled experiences that could be recreated as immersive ones, and related them to each other.
Specification
Dimensions, highs and lows.They dimensionalised each experience and tagged its highs and lows. This set the mood.
Build
A world, deployed.They built the world, rendered it with immersive tools, and deployed it on Meta headsets.
What the students named

What it taught me

The way of teaching that emerged in the room was stronger than the one I had planned. The students owned the framework because they discovered it.
