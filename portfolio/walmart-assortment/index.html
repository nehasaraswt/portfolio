Walmart's assortment was a manual, intricate dance: which items, how many, and the nuance of each store. With a pandemic and an organization in change, even the merchant's role wasn't commonly understood.

I joined the India extension of Valerie Casey's design team, and led design in a four-in-the-box with product, engineering and the business.

What we found
Demand, supply, pricing and logistics all depend on each other.
Buyers need information in sequence.
Merchants' work with suppliers stays human.
Aid, then decide.The product balanced helping merchants decide with deciding for them, so merchants stayed in charge.
Sequence the information.We brought scattered data into streamlined tools, in the order buyers work, across space, pricing, markdowns and adjacencies.
Change a running system gently.We redefined roles with the business, and kept checking in with merchants for comfort and new skills.
