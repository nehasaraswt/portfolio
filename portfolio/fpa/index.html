Desktop customers wouldn't move to the cloud because it had no planning, and Online customers were planning elsewhere. This new product builds the bridge for them. Launched in four months, GA in six.

We designed for Intuit Assist from the start; FP&A was the first product to ship with it.

The signal
85%of a ~8M customer base planned in spreadsheets.
40%of Desktop customers (680K) planned regularly
8%of Online customers (408K) exported reports to plan elsewhere
Change the customer.Research moved us from young businesses (2–5 years) to steady and expanding ones (5–10+), where interest and buying power were higher.
Change the shape.From planning sliced into each SKU, to one add-on across all of them, at $85–130 a month.
Release small, often.We shipped in small, repeated releases to grow adoption.
